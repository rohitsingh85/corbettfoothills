# GBP Content Strategy — Corbett Foothills Retreat

## Posting Cadence
- **Frequency:** 3–4 posts per week (Mon, Wed, Fri + optional Sat)
- **Best times:** 9–11 AM and 5–7 PM (IST)
- **Mix ratio:** 40% photo posts, 30% video/reel posts, 20% offer/event posts, 10% user-generated content

---

## Content Categories

### 1. Guest Experience Photos
**Goal:** Show the quality of personalised hospitality
- Warm welcome shots (refreshment tray, check-in moment)
- Evening bonfire with guests
- Staff interacting with guests (meal service, safari briefing)
- Candid guest moments on verandas or garden paths
- **Sample caption:** "Every guest arrives as a stranger and leaves as family. Here's to unhurried evenings, warm conversation, and the kind of hospitality that remembers your name."
- **Posting frequency:** 1 per week

### 2. Pet-Friendly Stay Photos
**Goal:** Drive bookings from pet-owners (high-intent segment)
- Dogs running on the lawn
- Guests walking their pets along the forest edge
- Pets relaxing on room verandas
- Pets interacting with farm animals
- **Sample caption:** "No kennels. No restrictions. No extra charges. At Corbett Foothills Retreat, your dog is as welcome as you are — with open lawns, forest walks, and the kind of holiday every pet deserves."
- **Posting frequency:** 1x every 2 weeks (plus seasonal boosts)

### 3. Freshly Cooked Food Photos
**Goal:** Differentiate from resort buffets
- Plated dishes (close-ups with good lighting)
- Chef preparing a meal in the open kitchen
- Ingredients sourced from the organic farm (just-picked vegetables, herbs)
- Kumaoni dishes in traditional serveware
- **Sample caption:** "This morning's harvest became tonight's dinner. From garden to plate in under two hours — no buffet, no heat lamps, no shortcuts. Just food that tastes like the foothills."
- **Posting frequency:** 1x every 2 weeks

### 4. Scenic Drive-to-Property Photos
**Goal:** Showcase location and journey, build anticipation
- Forest road approaching the property
- Rural village scenery en route
- Kosi River crossing
- Property exterior emerging from trees
- **Sample caption:** "The last 2 kilometres of your drive — where the road narrows, the air changes, and city sounds fade into forest silence. You've almost arrived."
- **Posting frequency:** 1x per month

### 5. Wildlife and Birdlife Photos
**Goal:** Establish connection to Corbett ecosystem
- Birds spotted on the property (barbets, kingfishers, woodpeckers)
- Wildlife from nearby safari zones (elephants, deer, tigers — from guest or guide photos)
- Butterfly and insect macro photography
- Forest landscape during golden hour
- **Sample caption:** "The forest doesn't begin at the park gate. At our property, the wild comes to the edge of your room. This spotted owlet visits every evening — a reminder that we are guests here too."
- **Posting frequency:** 1x per week

### 6. Family Experience Photos
**Goal:** Appeal to family travellers
- Children feeding goats or collecting eggs
- Kids walking through the organic farm
- Family mealtime (family-style dining setup)
- Parents with children at the bonfire or garden
- **Sample caption:** "The holidays children remember forever aren't about swimming pools or game rooms. They're about the goat that ate from their hand, the tomato they picked for dinner, and the starry sky they fell asleep under."
- **Posting frequency:** 1x per week

## UGC Strategy
- Encourage guests to tag @corbettfoothillsretreat in their stories
- Repost best 1–2 per week with a thank-you caption
- Run a quarterly "Best Corbett Memory" contest (prize: a free weekend stay)
- Ask for Google Reviews after checkout via follow-up message

## Posting Checklist
- [ ] Photo resolution ≥ 1080px wide
- [ ] First sentence hooks within 125 characters (visible without "more")
- [ ] Single line break between paragraphs for readability
- [ ] 1–2 relevant hashtags (e.g., #CorbettFoothillsRetreat, #PetFriendlyCorbett)
- [ ] Location tag: Corbett Foothills Retreat
- [ ] Call to action in last line: "Book your stay" / "Plan your visit" / "Link in bio"
