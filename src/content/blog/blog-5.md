## Two Zones, Two Experiences

Jim Corbett National Park is divided into multiple tourism zones, each offering a distinct safari experience. For guests staying at Corbett Foothills Retreat, two zones stand out as the most accessible and rewarding: Dhela and Jhirna. Both are located within easy driving distance of the retreat, but they offer markedly different landscapes, wildlife encounters, and atmospheric qualities.

## Dhela Safari Zone

Opened to the public in 2014, Dhela is the newest tourism zone in the Corbett Tiger Reserve. Covering approximately 1,100 hectares, it was the first zone to be notified as a buffer area before being upgraded to a full tourism destination.

### Landscape

Dhela is characterised by sprawling open grasslands interspersed with dense thickets of khair and sal regeneration. The topography is relatively flat, offering long sightlines across the meadows. This open terrain makes Dhela the preferred zone for wildlife photography — golden light across the grasslands at dawn creates spectacular compositions.

### Wildlife Highlights

Dhela's open grasslands support a high density of Bengal tiger sightings, particularly during the summer months when the grass is shorter. The zone is also excellent for:

- **Elephants** — Small herds are frequently seen crossing the grassland clearings.
- **Spotted deer and sambar** — The primary prey base is abundant and visible.
- **Wild boar** — Commonly seen rooting in the softer soil near waterholes.
- **Sloth bear** — A special sighting in the denser thicket areas.
- **Birdlife** — Grassland specialists including the Sarus Crane, Bengal Florican, and various lark and pipit species.

### Best Time to Visit

Dhela is open from October to June. The ideal months are March through May, when the grass is low and animals congregate near remaining water sources. Early morning safaris offer the best light and activity.

### Safari Options

Dhela offers both gypsy (jeep) and canter (large vehicle) safaris. Gypsies are recommended for photography enthusiasts, as they can access narrower trails and stop more freely.

## Jhirna Safari Zone

Jhirna is one of the oldest tourism zones in Corbett, having been open to visitors since the 1990s. It is also one of the few zones that remains open year-round, making it a reliable option during the monsoon season when most other zones are closed.

### Landscape

Unlike Dhela's open grasslands, Jhirna is dominated by dense sal forests interspersed with flowing streams, rocky outcrops, and narrow valleys. The canopy cover is significantly thicker, creating a more enclosed, atmospheric experience. Sunlight filters through the sal leaves in dappled patterns, and the sound of cicadas forms a constant backdrop.

### Wildlife Highlights

Jhirna's dense forest cover makes wildlife sightings more challenging but potentially more rewarding:

- **Tiger** — Sightings require patience, but Jhirna has a resident population of tigers well-adapted to the dense terrain.
- **Leopard** — More frequently sighted here than in any other Corbett zone.
- **Elephant** — Regular sightings, often encountered on the forest trails.
- **Sloth bear** — Jhirna is one of the best zones in India for sloth bear sightings.
- **Reptiles** — Monitor lizards, pythons, and the occasional king cobra.
- **Avian life** — Excellent for forest bird species: Great Hornbill, woodpeckers, leafbirds, and minivets.

### Best Time to Visit

Jhirna's year-round accessibility makes it ideal for last-minute safari bookings. The monsoon months (July–September) offer a unique experience with lush green foliage, flowing streams, and fewer tourists. Winter mornings can be foggy, adding a mystical quality to the forest.

## Which Zone Should You Choose?

The short answer: both. Each zone offers a distinctly different interpretation of the Corbett wilderness. A two-safari itinerary — one in Dhela and one in Jhirna — provides a comprehensive experience of the park's ecological diversity.

| Factor | Dhela | Jhirna |
|--------|-------|--------|
| Terrain | Open grasslands | Dense sal forest |
| Tiger density | High | Moderate |
| Photography | Excellent | Challenging but rewarding |
| Year-round access | Oct–Jun | All year |
| Best for | First-time visitors, photographers | Experienced safari-goers, birders |
