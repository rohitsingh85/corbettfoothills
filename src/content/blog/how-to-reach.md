Corbett Foothills Retreat is situated in the village of Gaibua Khas, Bail Parao, on the outskirts of Ramnagar in the Nainital district of Uttarakhand. The property is positioned at the very edge of the Corbett forest, offering guests uninterrupted views of the wilderness and immediate access to nature trails, birdwatching spots, and the boundary of Jim Corbett National Park.

The retreat is only 8 kilometres from the main Ramnagar town centre and an equal distance from the entry gates of Jim Corbett National Park. This proximity makes it one of the most conveniently located boutique stays in the region — close enough for easy access to safari zones and local amenities, yet far enough from the town's bustle to offer genuine peace and quiet.

## How to Reach from Delhi

Delhi is the most common starting point for travellers heading to Corbett Foothills Retreat. The journey is straightforward with multiple transport options available.

### By Car

Driving from Delhi to Corbett Foothills Retreat is the most flexible and scenic option. The distance is approximately 260 kilometres and typically takes 5.5 to 6.5 hours depending on traffic and road conditions.

The recommended route is Delhi → Ghaziabad → Hapur → Moradabad → Kashipur → Ramnagar → Bail Parao. The roads are good all the way, with the final stretch from Kashipur to Ramnagar offering beautiful views of the Kumaon foothills. The last 8 kilometres from Ramnagar to the retreat pass through quiet country roads along the forest edge.

### By Train

Ramnagar Railway Station is the closest railhead, located just 8 kilometres from the retreat. Several trains connect Delhi to Ramnagar, making this a convenient and comfortable option. The most popular trains include the Ranikhet Express (overnight journey arriving early morning), the Corbett Park Link Express, and several state-run express trains that connect Delhi to Kathgodam.

### By Bus

Regular bus services operate from Delhi's Anand Vihar ISBT and Kashmere Gate ISBT to Ramnagar. Both government-run UPSRTC/UTC buses and private operators offer services including standard, semi-sleeper, and sleeper coaches. The bus journey takes approximately 7 to 8 hours.

## Nearest Railway Stations

**Ramnagar Railway Station** is the most convenient railhead, located just 8 kilometres from the retreat. It is well-connected with direct trains from Delhi. The drive from the station to the retreat takes approximately 15 to 20 minutes through scenic roads along the forest edge.

**Kathgodam Railway Station** is the major railway terminus for the Kumaon region, located approximately 70 kilometres from the retreat. It is well-connected to Delhi, Lucknow, and other major cities. From Kathgodam, taxis are readily available for the 1.5-hour drive.

**Haldwani Railway Station** (65 km) and **Kashipur Junction** (40 km) are additional alternatives that can be useful during peak tourist seasons.

## Nearest Airports

**Pantnagar Airport (PGH)** is the closest airport, located approximately 65 kilometres away. It operates scheduled flights from Delhi operated by Alliance Air. The flight duration is approximately 1 hour. From the airport, taxis are available for the 1.5-hour drive to the retreat.

**Indira Gandhi International Airport, Delhi (DEL)** serves as the primary gateway for international travellers. From Delhi Airport, the retreat is approximately 260 kilometres away — a 5.5 to 6.5-hour drive.

## Driving Directions: Step by Step

1. **Delhi to Moradabad** (160 km, 3–3.5 hrs) — Take NH-9 via Ghaziabad and Hapur. Well-maintained highway with multiple toll booths and roadside amenities.

2. **Moradabad to Kashipur** (50 km, 1 hr) — Continue on NH-9 towards Kashipur. The landscape begins to change as you approach the Kumaon region.

3. **Kashipur to Ramnagar** (30 km, 45 min) — Pass through green landscapes with occasional forest cover. One of the most scenic stretches of the journey.

4. **Ramnagar to Corbett Foothills Retreat** (8 km, 15–20 min) — Head towards Bail Parao. The final approach road runs along the forest edge. Drive carefully, especially during dawn and dusk.

## Best Time to Travel

The most popular period is **October to February**, when the weather is pleasant with daytime temperatures between 15°C and 25°C. Birdwatching is at its peak with numerous migratory species in the region.

**March to June** offers excellent wildlife sightings as animals gather near water sources. The chances of tiger sightings are higher, and accommodation availability is better.

**July to September** brings the monsoon season, transforming the landscape into a lush green paradise. Crowds are minimal, rates are lower, and the atmosphere is peaceful. Some safari zones may have limited access during this period.

For a detailed seasonal guide, read our blog on the [best time to visit Jim Corbett National Park](/blogs/best-time-to-visit-jim-corbett-national-park).

## Nearby Attractions on the Route

**Garjiya Devi Temple** — Perched on a rock formation beside the Kosi River, about 12 kilometres from the retreat. Particularly scenic during sunrise and sunset.

**Corbett Museum** — Located in Kaladhungi, approximately 20 kilometres from the retreat. Former residence of Jim Corbett, showcasing personal belongings and stories from his legendary man-eater hunts.

**Local Markets in Ramnagar** — Just 8 kilometres from the retreat, offering souvenirs, local handicrafts, and fresh produce.

## Travel Tips for Families

If driving, plan for breaks every two hours. The stretch between Moradabad and Kashipur has several family-friendly dhabas and restaurants. Carry snacks, water, and entertainment for children.

For families taking the train, the overnight Ranikhet Express is a popular choice as children can sleep during the journey. We are happy to [arrange a pickup](/contact) upon prior request.

## Travel Tips for Guests with Pets

If travelling by car, plan for frequent breaks to allow your pet to stretch and hydrate. Carry a sufficient supply of your pet's regular food, a portable water bowl, a leash, and familiar bedding.

For guests arriving by train, check the Indian Railways pet policy before booking. Pets are allowed in certain classes with prior registration.

Corbett Foothills Retreat is a pet-friendly property. Our [pet-friendly accommodation](/accommodation) and open grounds provide a wonderful environment for pets to explore.
