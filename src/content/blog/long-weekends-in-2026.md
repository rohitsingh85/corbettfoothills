For many travellers, the best trips are not the longest ones — they are the well-planned escapes that fit neatly into a long weekend.

The year 2026 offers several extended weekends spread across the seasons, creating opportunities to trade city traffic for forest trails, mountain views, riverside walks, and meaningful time with family, friends, or even pets.

For travellers from Delhi NCR and nearby regions, Jim Corbett and the surrounding Kumaon foothills remain one of the most accessible destinations for a short nature retreat. Whether you're interested in wildlife safaris, birdwatching, village experiences, scenic drives, local food, or simply slowing down for a few days, the long weekends of 2026 provide plenty of reasons to plan ahead.

## January 2026

### New Year Peak

**1 January – 4 January 2026**

The first long weekend of the year arrives immediately after New Year's celebrations. Winter is one of the most pleasant times to visit the Corbett landscape, with cool mornings, clear skies, and excellent wildlife viewing conditions.

**Things to do**

- Early morning jungle safaris
- Riverside walks
- Winter birdwatching
- Bonfires and outdoor dining
- Exploring nearby villages

Travellers interested in wildlife often find winter one of the most rewarding seasons, as animals are easier to spot around water sources and open grasslands.

### Republic Day Weekend

**24 January – 26 January 2026**

A classic three-day escape from Delhi NCR.

This is an ideal time for first-time visitors to explore the region and experience some of the activities covered in our guide to [Top Things to Do Near Jim Corbett](/blogs/things-to-do-jim-corbett).

**Recommended activities**

- Jeep safaris
- Nature walks
- Farm experiences
- Corbett Museum visits
- Photography excursions

## February–March 2026

### Spring Birding Season

**February – March**

While not technically a long weekend, this period deserves special mention. Hundreds of resident and migratory bird species can be spotted across the foothills and riverine forests.

Bird enthusiasts may enjoy:

- Guided birding walks
- Riverside observation sessions
- Forest photography
- Exploring lesser-known trails away from tourist crowds

Travellers often discover that Corbett offers far more than tiger safaris alone.

### Holi Weekend

**1 March – 8 March 2026**

Holi creates one of the first major travel periods of the year.

The weather remains pleasant, forests are vibrant, and visitor numbers begin increasing before the summer rush.

This is a good time to combine:

- Wildlife experiences
- Family getaways
- Local cultural exploration
- Farm-to-table dining experiences

Families travelling with children may also enjoy reading about [why nature retreats often create stronger memories](/blogs/children-remember-nature-retreats-longer) than conventional resort holidays.

## Summer Holidays (May–June 2026)

### Delhi NCR School Holidays

**15 May – 30 June 2026**

Summer vacations represent one of the busiest travel periods around Corbett.

Contrary to popular belief, summer can be excellent for wildlife sightings. As temperatures rise, animals visit water sources more frequently, often improving viewing opportunities. For a deeper understanding of seasonal differences, read our guide on the [Best Time to Visit Jim Corbett National Park](/blogs/best-time-to-visit-jim-corbett-national-park).

Activities suitable for families include:

- Safari experiences
- River visits
- Village exploration
- Outdoor games
- Learning about local ecosystems

If you're planning your first safari, our [Complete Guide to Jim Corbett Safari Booking](/blogs/complete-guide-to-jim-corbett-safari-booking) can help avoid common mistakes.

### Muharram Long Weekend

**26 June – 29 June 2026**

A final opportunity for a summer getaway before the monsoon season begins.

The landscape starts transitioning from dry summer tones to lush green scenery, making it particularly attractive for photographers.

## Monsoon Long Weekends (July–September)

### Rath Yatra Weekend

**16 July – 19 July 2026**

The monsoon transforms the foothills into a dramatically different destination.

Waterfalls become active, forests turn emerald green, and the countryside feels refreshed.

Popular activities include:

- Scenic drives
- Village walks
- Photography
- Birdwatching
- Relaxed countryside stays

Many visitors are surprised by how enjoyable the monsoon season can be, especially when expectations extend beyond safari-focused travel.

### Independence Day Weekend

**15 August – 17 August 2026**

One of the most sought-after long weekends of the year.

The combination of lush landscapes and cooler temperatures makes this an excellent period for:

- Family vacations
- Couple getaways
- Pet-friendly travel
- Slow travel experiences

For travellers with pets, our [pet-friendly stay guide](/pet-friendly-stay) explains why Corbett Foothills Retreat is an ideal choice for animal companions.

### Onam / Milad Travel Window

**25 August – 30 August 2026**

This extended holiday period is ideal for travellers seeking quieter experiences away from peak festival crowds.

The surrounding foothills offer opportunities to:

- Explore local culture
- Enjoy nature walks
- Experience rural Kumaon
- Discover farm-based experiences

## Autumn and Peak Safari Season (October–November)

### Gandhi Jayanti Weekend

**2 October – 4 October 2026**

October marks the beginning of one of the most popular seasons around Corbett.

The weather improves significantly after the monsoon, visibility increases, and wildlife activity becomes more predictable.

### Dussehra Travel Period

**17 October – 20 October 2026**

By mid-October, visitor demand rises sharply.

This period is ideal for:

- Wildlife enthusiasts
- Photography groups
- Family gatherings
- Short festival getaways

Travellers looking for a quieter and more personal experience often [compare smaller nature-focused stays with larger resorts](/blogs/farmstay-vs-resort) before planning their trip.

### Diwali Peak Period

**6 November – 11 November 2026**

One of the busiest travel windows in India.

Advance planning is highly recommended, especially for:

- Accommodation
- Safari permits
- Transportation

The pleasant weather, festive atmosphere, and excellent wildlife conditions make this one of the most rewarding times to visit the region.

### Guru Nanak Weekend

**21 November – 24 November 2026**

The forests are at their most comfortable during late November.

Visitors can enjoy:

- Safaris
- Nature walks
- Outdoor dining
- Scenic drives through the foothills

## Winter Escapes (December 2026)

### Winter Wildlife Season

**1 December – 24 December 2026**

Many experienced travellers consider winter the most balanced season to visit Corbett.

Advantages include:

- Comfortable temperatures
- Excellent visibility
- Active birdlife
- Pleasant outdoor conditions

Those interested in understanding seasonal differences can explore our detailed guide on the [Best Time to Visit Jim Corbett National Park](/blogs/best-time-to-visit-jim-corbett-national-park).

### Christmas & New Year Peak

**25 December 2026 – 3 January 2027**

The year's final holiday period also becomes one of its busiest.

Families, couples, wildlife enthusiasts, and pet parents all head toward nature destinations during this time.

Early reservations are strongly recommended.

## Planning Your Long Weekend in Corbett

One reason Jim Corbett continues to attract repeat visitors is the variety of experiences available throughout the year.

A three-day trip can look completely different depending on the season:

- **Winter** brings birdwatching and comfortable safaris.
- **Summer** offers excellent wildlife sightings.
- **Monsoon** reveals lush landscapes and quiet countryside experiences.
- **Autumn** delivers some of the year's best wildlife and photography opportunities.

Whether you're planning a family holiday, a pet-friendly getaway, a wildlife-focused escape, or simply a break from city life, the long weekends of 2026 provide multiple opportunities to experience the forests, rivers, villages, and foothills of Kumaon at their best.

The smartest travellers often plan these weekends several months in advance — not only to secure safari permits and accommodation, but also to experience the region at a slower pace, rather than rushing through a crowded holiday itinerary.
