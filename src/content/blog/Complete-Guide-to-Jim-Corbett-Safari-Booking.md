## Why Visit Jim Corbett National Park?

Established in 1936 as India's first national park, Jim Corbett National Park spans over 500 square kilometres of forests, grasslands, rivers, and hills in Uttarakhand.

The park is home to:
- Bengal Tigers
- Asian Elephants
- Leopards
- Sloth Bears
- Crocodiles
- Deer Species
- More than 600 bird species

A safari through Corbett offers a rare opportunity to witness these magnificent creatures in their natural environment while enjoying some of North India's most beautiful forest landscapes.

## Understanding Jim Corbett Safari Zones

The park is divided into several tourism zones, each offering unique landscapes and wildlife experiences.

### Dhikala Zone

Dhikala is often considered the crown jewel of Jim Corbett National Park. Famous for its vast grasslands, riverbeds, and high wildlife density, it offers some of the best opportunities for tiger and elephant sightings.

**Highlights:**
- Largest safari zone
- Excellent tiger sightings
- Large elephant herds
- Spectacular landscapes
- Overnight forest rest houses available

**Best For:** Wildlife enthusiasts, photographers, and serious tiger seekers.

### Bijrani Zone

Located close to Ramnagar, Bijrani is among the most popular safari zones due to its accessibility and excellent wildlife viewing opportunities.

**Highlights:**
- Dense forests and open grasslands
- Good tiger movement
- Rich birdlife
- Scenic forest tracks

**Best For:** First-time visitors and families.

### Jhirna Zone

Jhirna remains open throughout the year, making it a popular choice during seasons when other zones may be closed.

**Highlights:**
- Year-round accessibility
- Good wildlife sightings
- Deer, elephants, and sloth bears frequently spotted

**Best For:** Visitors travelling during the [monsoon season](/blogs/best-time-to-visit-jim-corbett-national-park).

### Dhela Zone

Dhela combines dense forests with open grasslands and has become increasingly popular among wildlife photographers.

**Highlights:**
- Diverse landscapes
- Healthy wildlife population
- Less crowded than some popular zones

**Best For:** Nature lovers seeking a quieter safari experience.

### Garjia Zone

One of the newer safari zones, Garjia offers picturesque landscapes and growing wildlife activity.

**Highlights:**
- Beautiful forest scenery
- Relatively fewer vehicles
- Increasing tiger movement

**Best For:** Visitors looking for a less crowded safari experience.

### Sitabani Forest Area

Although technically not part of the core tiger reserve, Sitabani remains popular for nature walks, birdwatching, and scenic forest drives.

**Highlights:**
- Rich birdlife
- Spiritual significance
- Beautiful forest environment

**Best For:** Families, birdwatchers, and photographers.

## Jim Corbett Safari Pricing (2026)

Safari costs vary depending on the zone, vehicle type, and visitor nationality.

Typical expenses include:
- Forest entry fees
- Vehicle charges
- Guide fees
- Online booking charges

For Indian visitors, a standard jeep safari generally costs between ₹4,000 and ₹8,000 per vehicle, depending on the zone and season.

Canter safaris, available primarily in the Dhikala Zone, are usually more economical per person.

## How to Book a Jim Corbett Safari

Booking a safari is relatively straightforward if done in advance.

**Step 1: Select Your Preferred Zone**

Research the various safari zones and decide which one best suits your interests. If tiger sightings are your primary goal, Dhikala and Bijrani are often preferred.

**Step 2: Choose Your Safari Date**

Safari permits are limited and can sell out quickly, especially during weekends, holidays, and peak tourist seasons. Booking several weeks in advance is strongly recommended.

**Step 3: Provide Visitor Details**

The forest department requires:
- Full name
- Age
- Gender
- Valid identification details

The original ID used during booking must be carried during the safari.

**Step 4: Complete Payment**

Once payment is processed and permits are confirmed, your safari booking is secured.

## When Should You Book Your Safari?

For the best availability:
- **Peak Season (October–February):** Book 30–60 days in advance.
- **Long Weekends and Holidays:** Book even earlier.
- **Summer Season (March–June):** Book 15–30 days in advance.
- **Monsoon Season (July–September):** Availability is generally easier.
