## 1. Explore Jim Corbett National Park

No visit to Ramnagar is complete without experiencing India's oldest national park. Spread across dense forests, grasslands, riverbeds, and hills, Jim Corbett National Park is renowned for its rich biodiversity and wildlife conservation efforts.

Visitors can enjoy thrilling [jeep safaris](/experiences) through designated zones, offering opportunities to spot elephants, deer, wild boars, crocodiles, and hundreds of bird species. The park is also famous for being home to the majestic Bengal tiger.

A [safari through Corbett](/blogs/complete-guide-to-jim-corbett-safari-booking) is an unforgettable experience for wildlife enthusiasts, photographers, and families alike.

## 2. Enjoy Bird Watching and Nature Photography in Corbett Region

The forests and riverbanks surrounding Ramnagar are a paradise for bird lovers. The region is home to [hundreds of resident and migratory bird species](/blogs/best-time-to-visit-jim-corbett-national-park), making it one of North India's premier birdwatching destinations.

Early mornings are particularly rewarding, when the forest comes alive with birdsong and activity. Whether you're an experienced birder or simply enjoy observing nature, birdwatching is a peaceful and enriching experience.

## 4. Visit Most Popular Religious Places Near Ramnagar

### a. Garjiya Devi Temple

Located on the banks of the Kosi River, Garjiya Devi Temple is one of the most revered spiritual landmarks in the region. Perched atop a large rock formation, the temple offers stunning river views and a peaceful atmosphere.

### b. Hanuman Dham

Situated close to Ramnagar town, this is a modern temple complex dedicated to Lord Hanuman. Known for its large idol and well-maintained premises, the temple is popular among local families and visitors.

### c. Sitabani Temple

Associated with the legend that Goddess Sita spent part of her exile here, this temple is located within the scenic Sitabani forest area. Popular among pilgrims and nature lovers alike, the place offers a peaceful alternative to the more crowded temples.

### d. Kainchi Dham

This place is famous as the ashram of Neem Karoli Baba. Visited by devotees from around the world, the ashram is known for its serene atmosphere and spiritual significance and can be reached in about 2–2.5 hours drive from Ramnagar.

### e. Naina Devi Temple

One of the Shakti Peeths of India, the temple is located on the banks of Naini Lake. Often combined with a day trip to Nainital, this place is roughly 2 hours away from Ramnagar.

## 5. Experience the Beauty of the Kosi River

Flowing through the Corbett landscape, the Kosi River adds to the region's natural charm. Visitors can spend time along the riverbanks, enjoy scenic views, watch birds, or simply relax amidst the tranquil surroundings.

The river is especially beautiful during sunrise and sunset, when the changing light creates spectacular reflections across the water.

## 8. Enjoy Forest Mornings and Sunset Views

Sometimes the most memorable travel experiences require no planning at all. Wake up to the sounds of birds, enjoy a cup of tea surrounded by nature, and watch the changing colours of the sky as the sun rises and sets over the forest landscape.

## 9. Taste the Mountains, One Traditional Recipe at a Time

A trip to Uttarakhand is incomplete without tasting its rich mountain cuisine, shaped by generations of tradition and local ingredients. Beyond the forests and hills lies a culinary heritage that is simple, wholesome, and deeply connected to the land.

Savour local delicacies like Aloo Ke Gutke, Bhatt Ki Churkani, Dubke, Kumaoni Raita, Chainsoo, Mutton Bhutuwa and Jhangora Kheer for an authentic mountain dining experience.
